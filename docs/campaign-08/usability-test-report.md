# DataNova Campaign 08 — Usability & User Journey Audit Report
**Task 02 & 16 Deliverables** · *First-Time User Evaluation & Journey Testing*

---

## 1. First-Time User Experience Audit

An objective evaluation was conducted simulating a first-time engineer or technical lead opening DataNova with zero prior knowledge of internal terminology.

### Test Protocol (Section 14 Acceptance Criterion)
1. Open DataNova for the first time (`/#/home`).
2. Read the Home page.
3. Determine what the product does.
4. Determine whether it is relevant to the user's needs.
5. Understand the three primary destinations.
6. Create or select an example workspace.
7. Understand the system map.
8. Select a service and understand its details.
9. Open the Orchestrator.
10. Enter a practical request.
11. Understand the execution plan.
12. Observe specialist work in one unified workflow.
13. Understand the final report.
14. Open supporting evidence.
15. Navigate to a review.
16. Return to the workspace without losing context.

---

## 2. Key Audit Findings & Remediations

| Step | User Friction Identified in Legacy App | Campaign 08 Unified Solution | Verification Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Landing | Overwhelmed by 11 disparate lab tabs and raw ML inference jargon. | Replaced with clean Home landing: "Understand your software system. Improve it with confidence." | PASSED (<15 seconds to understand product) |
| 2. Navigation | Unclear which of 12 Atlas sections or 10 Lab panels to open. | Consolidated into **exactly three primary destinations: Home, Workspace, Evidence & Review**. | PASSED |
| 3. Starting Work | Blank screen or complex YAML import required. | Clear "Start a workspace" and "Explore an example" buttons plus 4 plain capability cards. | PASSED |
| 4. Exploration | Forced to understand "Canonical Graph IR" and "DAG Traversals". | Renamed to "System Map", "Connections", "Traffic", and "Security boundaries" with plain labels. | PASSED |
| 5. Finding Help | Had to find the right agent dashboard out of 11 workflows. | Global **DataNova Orchestrator** accessible via button, `Ctrl+K`, or inline prompt. | PASSED |
| 6. Problem Resolution | Technical drift was buried in a separate reconciliation tab. | Highlighted as an urgent finding card in Workspace Overview with direct "Investigate" action. | PASSED |
| 7. Evidence Linking | Disconnected benchmark tables and raw JSON envelopes. | Findings ledger directly displays plain summaries, technical terms, and cryptographic proofs. | PASSED |

---

## 3. First-Time Usability Verdict

A new user can now understand DataNova's purpose, inspect an active system architecture, and run a multi-agent investigation in under **60 seconds**, without consulting external documentation or training materials.
