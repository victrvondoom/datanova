## Rules

- Unknown features fail generation (the registry may never silently drift from the artifact schema).
- Categorical one-hot indicators (`ch_*`, `cat_*`) are documented by rule; their vocabulary comes from the training partition only.
- All rolling features are strictly causal (prior events only).
